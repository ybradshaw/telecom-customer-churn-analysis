## Excel Analysis Findings

| Table | Findings | Recommendation | Team |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pivot Table Contract | Month-to-month customers have the highest churn rate at 51.69%, compared with 10.88% for one-year contracts and 2.58% for two-year contracts. | Test targeted retention offers and incentives to encourage month-to-month customers to switch to longer-term contracts. | Marketing |
| Pivot Table Tenure | Customers with 0–9 months of tenure have the highest churn rate (66.45%), followed by customers with 10–19 months of tenure (32.16%). | Investigate the main reasons for early-stage churn and introduce targeted onboarding and engagement initiatives during the first months of the customer journey. | Customer Success, Marketing |
| Pivot Table Internet Type | Fiber Optic customers have a churn rate of 42.13%, indicating a segment that may require further investigation. | Analyze churn reasons among Fiber Optic customers and investigate potential issues with pricing, service quality, and customer support before defining targeted retention actions. | Product, Customer Support |
| Pivot Table Age | Customers aged 65+ have a churn rate of 42.81%, compared with 25.43% for customers under 65 — a difference of 17.38 percentage points. The 65+ group also has relatively few newly joined customers (30 out of 1,142). | Investigate the main churn drivers among customers aged 65+ and assess potential barriers to acquisition. Test targeted retention initiatives and evaluate their impact on churn rate. | Marketing, Customer Support |
| Pivot Table Monthly Charge | Customers paying $70–$79 per month have the highest churn rate (42.77%) and the largest number of churned customers (358). Churn rates are also elevated in the $80–$109 ranges (33.33%–38.61%). Additionally, churned customers have a higher average monthly charge ($73.35) than customers who stayed ($61.74), a difference of approximately 18.8%. | Investigate potential pricing, service, and perceived-value issues among customers paying $70–$109 per month. Prioritize retention efforts based on both monthly charges and churn risk, and measure their impact on churn rate and retained monthly revenue. | Product, Marketing, Customer Support |
